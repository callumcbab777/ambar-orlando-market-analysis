Plan your visit · Kissimmee, Florida

All Roads Lead to AMBAR

Fly into one of three airports, ride Brightline up from Miami, or drive in. AMBAR sits on W Irlo Bronson Memorial Hwy in Kissimmee, minutes from Walt Disney World. Here’s how to get here, when to come, and how to see the residences in person.

01 · Fly

Three airports, one destination

Orlando International (MCO) has the most direct flights, including from the UK. Orlando Sanford (SFB) and Melbourne Orlando (MLB) are smaller and further out, with mostly US routes plus TUI’s seasonal UK flights into Melbourne. No fares are shown here; use the Google Flights links for live prices.

02 · Ride

From Miami or South Florida: Brightline

Brightline’s intercity trains link MiamiCentral with its Orlando station at Orlando International Airport (MCO), stopping along the coast. From the Orlando station it’s about 27 miles, about 42 min by car to AMBAR with no traffic.

  1. OrlandoAt Orlando International Airport (MCO), 10705 Jeff Fuqua Blvd · closest to AMBAR
